Where a transaction goes

One transfer, followed from your wallet to Ethereum. Every stop is a real contract you can check.

  1. You sign

    Your wallet sends the transaction to ANT CHAIN and pays gas in ANT. No ETH needed on this chain.

    Chain ID26800

  2. The sequencer orders it

    ANT CHAIN's sequencer puts it in a block. Our first test transfer was confirmed in 2.7 seconds, measured end to end over the public internet.

    Latest block…

  3. A batch lands on Robinhood Chain

    Blocks are compressed into batches and posted to Robinhood Chain. The batch poster pays ETH for that and is paid back in ANT at a rate set from Chainlink ETH/USD and the ANT market price.

    Paid back at…

  4. A validator vouches for it

    A staked validator posts the resulting state. Anyone watching can dispute it during a challenge window of about 6.4 days. Withdrawals wait for that window.

    Validator…

  5. Ethereum has the final say

    Robinhood Chain settles to Ethereum, so ANT CHAIN inherits the same root of trust as every Arbitrum chain.

Moving ANT in and out

There is no bridge app yet. Until there is, these are the exact contract calls, the same ones we used to test the bridge.

Deposit credited in seconds

  1. Hold ANT on Robinhood Chain and a little ETH for gas there.
  2. Approve the ANT CHAIN inbox to spend your ANT.
  3. Call depositERC20(amount) on the inbox. The same amount appears on ANT CHAIN as gas.
# on Robinhood Chain (4663)
cast send 0xbbB6…B373 "approve(address,uint256)" \
  0x13be8403DdeA7826a6f46904344d4a9431c28Efa 5000000000000000
cast send 0x13be8403DdeA7826a6f46904344d4a9431c28Efa \
  "depositERC20(uint256)" 5000000000000000

Withdraw about 6.4 days

  1. On ANT CHAIN, call withdrawEth(destination) on ArbSys with the ANT amount as value. It burns on ANT CHAIN.
  2. Wait for the validator's assertion to pass the challenge window.
  3. Execute the message on the outbox on Robinhood Chain. Your ANT is released from the bridge.

The wait is the security model, not a delay we can shorten. Plan withdrawals ahead.

Built like any EVM chain

ANT CHAIN runs Arbitrum Nitro. Solidity, Foundry, Hardhat, viem and ethers work unchanged. The one difference: msg.value and balances are in ANT, not ETH.

  • Gas token is native, so address.balance is an ANT balance.
  • A standard CREATE2 deployer and Multicall are already deployed.
  • Bridged ERC-20s arrive through the standard gateway.
import { defineChain } from "viem";

export const antChain = defineChain({
  id: 26800,
  name: "ANT CHAIN",
  nativeCurrency: { name: "ANT", symbol: "ANT", decimals: 18 },
  rpcUrls: {
    default: { http: ["https://nitro-node-production.up.railway.app"] },
  },
});
# foundry.toml
[rpc_endpoints]
antchain = "https://nitro-node-production.up.railway.app"

# deploy

About the token you pay with

ANT CHAIN does not issue ANT and cannot change it. Here is what it is, and what its issuer can do.

Name on chain

Issuer
Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited
What it is
A tokenized debt security that tracks the share price. Holders get price exposure, not shares or shareholder rights.
Who can hold it
Not US persons. Also restricted in Canada, the UK, Switzerland, the UAE and sanctioned places. The issuer's terms decide.

What the issuer can do

  • Pause all ANT transfers. Deposits and withdrawals would stop while paused.
  • Block any address, including our bridge. ANT held there could not move.
  • Burn ANT from any address.
  • Upgrade the token contract for every stock token at once.
  • Adjust a multiplier after dividends or splits. On-chain balances stay the same; our fee price follows the market.

These are written into the token contract, which we read on chain. They are the same for every chain that uses a Robinhood stock token as gas.

Questions

Is ANT CHAIN made by Anthropic or Robinhood?

No. ANT CHAIN is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Anthropic, PBC, Robinhood Markets, Inc., or Offchain Labs. "ANT" is the ticker of the token used as gas.

Does ANT CHAIN have its own token?

No. There is no ANT CHAIN token, presale or airdrop. Anything claiming to be one is not ours. Several unrelated tokens use the ANT ticker, so always check the contract address listed above.

Why pay gas with a stock token?

Software agents spend most of their budget on AI compute. Pricing blockspace in a token that tracks an AI company keeps the unit of account close to what they buy. Fees are tiny either way: about a fortieth of a cent for a transfer at today's prices.

Why does withdrawing take about 6.4 days?

Withdrawals wait for the validator's claim about the chain's state to pass a challenge window, the same rule Arbitrum One uses. Deposits do not wait.

Who runs the chain?

One operator runs the sequencer, the batch poster and the validator today. That is normal for a new Arbitrum chain and it is the main trust assumption to know about. The validator posts a bond and its claims can be challenged.

Is there a block explorer?

Not yet. Until there is, any Ethereum tool pointed at the RPC URL can read blocks and transactions.
