How a fee moves

You see a dollar price. The chain collects ANT. Batches settle to Robinhood Chain, then Ethereum.

  1. You get a dollar quote

    An oracle converts the network fee into ANT at the current price, so wallets can show the cost in dollars before you sign.

  2. Gas is paid in ANT

    The fee is deducted from your ANT Stock Token balance. Apps can also sponsor fees through a paymaster, so users never touch gas at all.

  3. Batches settle downward

    ANT CHAIN posts batches to Robinhood Chain, which settles to Ethereum. Fraud proofs protect every step.

Built for agents that pay each other

One ant carries very little. A colony moves mountains. AI agents work the same way: many small calls, each needing a fast, cheap, verifiable payment.

Fast blocks
Targeting sub-second block times so an agent can pay for a call and get the answer in the same breath.
Session keys with limits
Give an agent a key that can only spend a set amount, on set contracts, for a set time. Revoke it any time.
Receipts on chain
Record which model answered, what it cost, and who paid. Anyone can check the receipt later.
Sponsored fees
Apps can pay gas for their users in ETH or stablecoins through a paymaster. The chain still settles in ANT.

What people can build

  • Pay-per-call APIs. Charge fractions of a cent for each inference request, settled instantly.
  • Agent marketplaces. Let agents hire other agents and pay on delivery.
  • Inference receipts. Prove which model produced an output and when.
  • Streaming payments. Pay for compute by the second instead of by the month.
  • Compute markets. Match idle GPUs with jobs and settle in one place.
  • Game economies. Run in-game trades with fees too small to notice.

EVM in, nothing new to learn

ANT CHAIN runs the Arbitrum Nitro stack. Foundry, Hardhat, viem and ethers work as they do on Ethereum. The only difference is that gas is denominated in ANT.

Questions

Is ANT CHAIN part of Anthropic?

No. ANT CHAIN is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Anthropic, PBC, Robinhood Markets, Inc., Offchain Labs, or any of their affiliates. "ANT" refers to the ticker of the tokenized stock used as gas.

What is the ANT Stock Token?

It is a token issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited on Robinhood Chain that tracks the price of Anthropic shares listed on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. It gives price exposure only. It is not a share and carries no shareholder rights.

Who can hold ANT Stock Tokens?

Eligibility follows the issuer's rules. Stock Tokens are not available to US persons and are restricted in other places, including Canada, the UK and Switzerland. Check the issuer's terms before you use them.

Why use a stock token as gas?

Agents spend most of their budget on AI compute. Pricing blockspace in a token tied to an AI company keeps the unit of account close to what agents are actually buying. Wallets still show every fee in dollars.

Does ANT CHAIN have its own coin?

No. There is no ANT CHAIN token, presale or airdrop. Any token claiming to be ours is not. Several unrelated tokens use the ANT ticker, so always check the issuer and contract address.

How do I know I have the real ANT Stock Token?

Check the contract address. The ANT Stock Token issued by Robinhood is 0xbbB6c29bCc3a1028A2B54310DF73F4F7A3bcB373 on Robinhood Chain, named "Anthropic, PBC Class A Common Stock • Robinhood Token". A token with the same ticker but a supply of one billion is an unrelated memecoin and cannot be bridged as gas.
