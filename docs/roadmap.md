# Roadmap

What is live today, what comes next, and what is planned. Statuses are kept up to date in this file and on [antchain.tech/roadmap](https://antchain.tech/roadmap/).

- **Live**: working now, try it.
- **Soon**: built or being built, opening next.
- **Planned**: intended, not promised. No dates until they are certain.

Nothing here is an offer or a promise of value.

## Not changing

- **Gas stays ANT.** $ANTC is for the chain's own economy, not a replacement for ANT as gas.
- **The mainnet challenge window stays about 7 days.** It is what protects funds in the bridge; fast withdrawals will come from liquidity providers instead.
- **ANT CHAIN is independent** of Anthropic, PBC, Robinhood Markets, Inc. and Offchain Labs.
