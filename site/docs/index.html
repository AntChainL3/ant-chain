Try the whole flow

  1. Connect. Open the bridge page and connect a wallet: MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, or a mobile wallet through WalletConnect.
  2. Get a test kit. Press Get test kit. You receive 100 tANT plus a little testnet ETH for gas on Robinhood Chain Testnet, once a day per wallet. No other faucet needed.

tANT, ANT and $ANTC are three different things

  • tANT: the free test gas token on testnet. No value.
  • ANT: the Robinhood Stock Token that is the gas token on mainnet. ANT CHAIN does not issue it.
  • $ANTC: the ANT CHAIN token, live on Pons. Contract 0x1A9ca5A347c4aeAb4870668E76506E45C23D6281.

Dollar amounts shown on testnet use the live ANT price only to show what fees would cost on mainnet.

What is ANT CHAIN

ANT CHAIN is an independent blockchain, a Layer 3 built with Arbitrum Orbit. It runs on top of Robinhood Chain, which itself settles to Ethereum. It behaves like any EVM chain: the same wallets, the same Solidity contracts, the same tools.

The one thing that makes it different is the token you pay gas with. On Ethereum you pay in ETH. On ANT CHAIN every fee is paid in ANT, the Robinhood stock token that tracks Anthropic, PBC shares (Nasdaq: ANT).

In one line

A fast, low-cost EVM chain where the gas token is Anthropic's ticker.

Independent

ANT CHAIN is not made, endorsed or sponsored by Anthropic, PBC, Robinhood Markets, Inc. or Offchain Labs. "ANT" is the ticker of the token used as gas.

Why gas is paid in ANT

Most of the next wave of on-chain activity will come from software: AI agents that pay for compute, data and each other. Those agents already budget in the economics of the AI industry. A chain whose unit of account tracks one of the companies building that industry keeps their costs and their gas in the same frame of reference.

  • One asset, two jobs. If you already hold ANT, you can use the same balance to pay for transactions instead of selling it for ETH first.
  • Tiny fees either way. A transfer costs about 28,400 gas at 0.1 gwei, roughly a fortieth of a cent at today's ANT price.
  • Real, not a wrapper. The gas is the ANT Stock Token itself, locked in the bridge on Robinhood Chain one to one. There is no synthetic version and no separate "gas coin".

Paying gas in ANT does not give you shares, votes or any rights in Anthropic. The ANT Stock Token gives price exposure only, under its issuer's terms (see About the ANT Stock Token).

How it works

  1. You sign. Your wallet sends a transaction to ANT CHAIN and pays gas in ANT.
  2. The sequencer orders it into a block within a second or two. Our first mainnet transfer was confirmed in 2.7 s end to end.
  3. A batch lands on Robinhood Chain. Blocks are compressed and posted to the sequencer inbox. The batch poster pays ETH for that and is repaid in ANT.
  4. A validator vouches for it. A bonded validator posts the resulting state using BoLD fraud proofs. Anyone can challenge a wrong claim during the challenge window: about 7 days on mainnet, about 30 minutes on testnet. Counting the batch and the claim itself, a testnet withdrawal can be claimed 45 to 60 minutes after it starts.
  5. Ethereum has the final say. Robinhood Chain settles to Ethereum, so ANT CHAIN shares its root of trust with every Arbitrum chain.

How fees are priced

Posting batches costs ETH on Robinhood Chain, but users pay in ANT. A fee pricer contract converts between them using Chainlink's ETH/USD feed and the ANT market price. A keeper updates the ANT price from the ANT pool on Robinhood Chain, with guards for low liquidity, stale data, large jumps, paused trading and corporate actions, and freezes rather than guessing when markets are closed or unusual.

Bridging ANT

Deposit: Robinhood Chain → ANT CHAIN

  1. Connect a regular wallet (an EOA). Deposits from smart-contract wallets arrive at an aliased address, so the bridge page blocks them.
  2. Approve the ANT CHAIN inbox to spend your ANT (first time only).
  3. Deposit. Your ANT is locked in the bridge contract on Robinhood Chain, and the same amount is credited as gas on ANT CHAIN at the same address, usually within a minute.

Withdrawal: ANT CHAIN → Robinhood Chain

  1. Start the withdrawal on ANT CHAIN. Your ANT is burned there and a message is created. Keep a little ANT for this transaction's gas.
  2. Wait for the challenge window: about 7 days on mainnet (50,400 Ethereum blocks), about an hour in total on testnet. The bridge page shows a countdown. This is the security model, not a delay we can shorten.
  3. Claim on Robinhood Chain from the bridge page. Your ANT is released from the bridge to you.
By hand

The bridge page calls approve and depositERC20(amount) on the inbox, withdrawEth(destination) on ArbSys, and executeTransaction(…) on the outbox. Exact commands are on the home page.

The exchange

The ANT CHAIN exchange runs the Uniswap V2 contracts, deployed unmodified from Uniswap's published bytecode, with Wrapped ANT (WANT) as the base asset. Anyone can create a pool, add liquidity and earn the 0.3% fee on trades through it.

  • ANT and WANT are the same asset. Wrapping is one to one and always reversible.
  • Pools start empty. The first deposit into a pool sets its starting price.
  • Check addresses. Anyone can create a token with any name. Trade only tokens whose contract you have verified.
Status

The exchange is live on testnet, with a seeded tANT / tUSD pool on the swap page. On mainnet it opens together with mainnet.

The $ANTC token Live

$ANTC is the ANT CHAIN token. It launched on Pons, the token launchpad on Robinhood Chain. Gas stays ANT; $ANTC is for the chain's own economy.

It has three jobs:

Express lane

Bid for priority ordering of your transactions in each round. Arbitrum's Timeboost runs a sealed-bid auction for an "express lane"; on ANT CHAIN the bids are paid in the ANT CHAIN token. Useful for traders and bots that need to land first.

Gas credits

Stake $ANTC on Robinhood Chain and earn gas credits every second (token-days). Credits pay for your transactions on ANT CHAIN once mainnet opens. Unstake any time. Live on the Stake page, contract 0xfd0d…b1ac.

Buyback and burn

A 0.01% fee on bridge deposits and swaps buys the ANT CHAIN token on the market and burns it. The fee and the burns are on chain for anyone to check.

What it is not

  • Not an Anthropic token. It has no connection to Anthropic, PBC, gives no rights in Anthropic or in ANT, and does nothing outside ANT CHAIN.
  • Only one address. 0x1A9ca5A347c4aeAb4870668E76506E45C23D6281 on Robinhood Chain is the only $ANTC. Anything else using the name is not ours.
  • Utilities arrive in steps. Staking for gas credits is live first; express-lane bids and the buyback come with ANT CHAIN mainnet.

Nothing on this page is an offer or a promise of value. Utilities describe how the token will be used on the chain, not what it will be worth.

Stake

  1. Connect. Open the Stake page and connect a wallet: MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, or a mobile wallet through WalletConnect.
  2. Switch network. The page asks your wallet to switch to Robinhood Chain and adds the network if needed.
  3. Enter an amount, or press Max.
  4. Approve, then stake. The first time there are two transactions: Approve, then Stake. Later stakes need only one.
  5. Watch credits grow. Your staked amount and credits update every second.

Credits and unstaking

  • Token-days. 1 $ANTC staked for 1 day earns 1 credit; 100 staked for 3 days earns 300.
  • Unstake any time. Use the Unstake tab: one transaction, no lock, no fee. Earned credits are kept.
  • Your tokens stay yours. Nobody, including the ANT CHAIN team, can move staked tokens or block an unstake. The owner can only pause new stakes and choose who records credit use.
  • Not a token. Credits cannot be transferred or sold and pay no yield. They are only a right to use gas on ANT CHAIN.

Trust model and risks

  • One operator today. A single operator runs the sequencer, the batch poster and the validator. That is normal for a new Arbitrum chain and the main thing you are trusting.
  • Censorship has a way out. If the sequencer ignores you, you can push a transaction through the delayed inbox on Robinhood Chain; it must be included after 4 days (28,800 Ethereum blocks).
  • Wrong state can be challenged. The validator posts a bond and its claims can be disputed with BoLD fraud proofs during the challenge window (~7 days on mainnet, ~30 minutes on testnet).
  • Upgrades are possible. The chain's contracts can be upgraded by its owner through the upgrade executor. There is no timelock or multisig yet.
  • The gas token has an issuer. Robinhood can pause, block, burn and upgrade ANT (see About the ANT Stock Token).
  • Beta software. The bridge and swap pages are new and unaudited interfaces over audited Arbitrum and Uniswap V2 contracts. Use amounts you can afford to have delayed.
  • Price risk. ANT tracks a share price. Your gas balance's dollar value moves with it.

FAQ

Do I need ETH on ANT CHAIN?

No. Gas on ANT CHAIN is ANT. You only need a little ETH on Robinhood Chain to make the deposit and, later, to claim a withdrawal.

How long does withdrawing take?

About 7 days on mainnet and about an hour on testnet. Withdrawals wait for the validator's claim to survive the challenge window, the same rule as Arbitrum One; testnet uses a short window so the whole flow can be tried. The bridge page shows a countdown. Deposits do not wait.

Can I bridge from a Safe or another smart wallet?

Not through the bridge page. Deposits from contracts are credited to an aliased address on ANT CHAIN. Use a regular wallet.

Does holding the ANT CHAIN token give me anything at Anthropic?

No. The token is only used on ANT CHAIN, for express-lane bids, gas allowances and the buyback. It has no connection to Anthropic.

What happens if ANT is paused by its issuer?

Transfers on Robinhood Chain stop, so deposits and claims stop too. ANT already on ANT CHAIN keeps working as gas there.

Is there a block explorer?

Yes. The ANT CHAIN Explorer shows blocks, transactions and addresses live from the chain, for mainnet and testnet.

Glossary

Layer 3 (L3)
A chain that settles to a Layer 2. ANT CHAIN settles to Robinhood Chain, which settles to Ethereum.
Sequencer
The service that orders transactions into blocks.
Batch poster
Posts compressed blocks to the parent chain so anyone can rebuild the chain.
Validator / BoLD
Posts claims about the chain's state with a bond; BoLD is Arbitrum's dispute protocol for challenging wrong claims.
Challenge window
The time a claim must survive before withdrawals it covers can be claimed: ~7 days on mainnet, ~30 minutes on testnet.
Fee token pricer
The contract that converts parent-chain costs (ETH) into ANT using Chainlink ETH/USD and the ANT price.
WANT
Wrapped ANT, the ERC-20 form of ANT used by the exchange. Always one to one.
Express lane (Timeboost)
A sealed-bid auction for priority ordering of transactions in each round.
