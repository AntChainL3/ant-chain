    Where a fee goes.

    1. Sign

      You sign, gas is ANT

      Your wallet sends the transaction to ANT CHAIN and pays gas in ANT. Balances, msg.value and fees are all in ANT, no ETH needed here.

      wallet / sign
$ send 0.001 tANT → 0x…
✓ chain id 26801
✓ fee paid in tANT

    2. Order

      The sequencer orders it

      ANT CHAIN's sequencer puts it in the next block, usually in about a second. Our first mainnet transfer confirmed in 2.7 seconds end to end.

      sequencer / order
$ eth_sendRawTransaction
→ block #…
✓ receipt returned

    3. Batch

      A batch lands on Robinhood Chain

      Blocks are compressed into batches and posted to Robinhood Chain. The poster pays ETH there and is paid back in ANT at a rate set from Chainlink ETH/USD and the ANT price.

      batch poster / robinhood chain
$ addSequencerL2Batch(…)
✓ batches posted: …
✓ repaid at …

    4. Vouch

      A validator vouches for it

      A bonded validator posts the chain's state. Anyone can dispute a wrong claim during the challenge window: about an hour on testnet, about 7 days on mainnet.

      validator / bold
$ assertion + bond
✓ validator: …
→ open to challenge

    5. Settle

      Ethereum has the final say

      Robinhood Chain settles to Ethereum, so ANT CHAIN shares the root of trust of every Arbitrum chain. Two hops, one root.

      settlement / ethereum
$ robinhood chain → ethereum
✓ final

    Know the token you pay with.

    ANT CHAIN does not issue ANT and cannot change it. Here is what it is, and what its issuer can do.

    Name on chain
    Anthropic, PBC Class A Common Stock • Robinhood Token
    Issuer
    Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited
    What it is
    A tokenized debt security that tracks the share price. Holders get price exposure, not shares or shareholder rights.
    Who can hold it
    Not US persons. Also restricted in Canada, the UK, Switzerland, the UAE and sanctioned places. The issuer's terms decide.
    • PauseAll ANT transfers. Deposits and withdrawals would stop while paused.
    • BlockAny address, including our bridge. ANT held there could not move.
    • BurnANT from any address.
    • UpgradeThe token contract, for every stock token at once.
    • AdjustA multiplier after dividends or splits. Balances stay the same; our fee price follows the market.

    faq Straight answers

    Questions people ask.

    Is ANT CHAIN made by Anthropic or Robinhood?

    No. ANT CHAIN is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Anthropic, PBC, Robinhood Markets, Inc., or Offchain Labs. "ANT" is the ticker of the token used as gas.

    Does ANT CHAIN have its own token?

    Yes: $ANTC, live on Pons at 0x1A9ca5A347c4aeAb4870668E76506E45C23D6281 on Robinhood Chain. Only this address is ours. It has three jobs on the chain: staking for gas credits (opening first), bidding for the express lane, and a 0.01% bridge and swap fee that buys it back and burns it (both with mainnet). Gas itself stays ANT. $ANTC is not connected to Anthropic, PBC. Nothing on this page is an offer.

    Why pay gas with a stock token?

    Software agents spend most of their budget on AI compute. Pricing blockspace in a token that tracks an AI company keeps the unit of account close to what they buy. Fees are tiny either way: a fraction of a cent for a transfer at today's prices.

    How long does withdrawing take?

    About an hour on testnet and about 7 days on mainnet. Withdrawals wait for the validator's claim about the chain's state to pass a challenge window, the same rule Arbitrum One uses; testnet uses a much shorter window so you can try the whole flow. The bridge page shows a countdown, and the Claim button appears when it ends. Deposits do not wait.

    Who runs the chain?

    One operator runs the sequencer, the batch poster and the validator today. That is normal for a new Arbitrum chain and it is the main trust assumption to know about. The validator posts a bond and its claims can be challenged.

    How do I get test tokens?

    Open the bridge, connect a wallet and press "Get test kit". It sends testnet ETH for gas and 100 tANT on Robinhood Chain Testnet, once a day per wallet. Test tokens have no value.
