How it moves

  1. Lock. Your ANT goes into the ANT CHAIN bridge contract on Robinhood Chain.
  2. Credit. The sequencer reads the deposit and credits the same amount as gas, usually within a minute.
  3. Withdraw. Burning ANT on ANT CHAIN creates a message. After a validator's claim survives the challenge window (about 30 minutes on testnet, so claims open roughly 45 to 60 minutes after you withdraw), you claim it on Robinhood Chain.
